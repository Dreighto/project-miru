   (line 284), placeholder race (line 318), spawn failure (line 343).

   cleared at startup (_loadFromDisk) and opportunistically during
   leaseSlot(). Lease entries include { trace_id, worker, leased_at, pid }.

   release/reclaim event. (No HTTP endpoint, but all state transitions
